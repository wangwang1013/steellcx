Privacy Policy

This notice explains how the website handles enquiries and technical requests during the current release.

Website Operator

This STEELLCX website is operated by 佛山市立诚兴不锈钢有限公司, a company registered in Foshan, mainland China. For privacy enquiries, contact sales@steellcx.com.

Information We Collect

If you send a quotation request, we receive your name, company (if provided), email address, WhatsApp or phone number (if provided), country or region, project type and project details. The website also receives technical request information needed to deliver and protect the service. This release does not store or read an optional tracking choice.

How We Use Information

We use enquiry details to respond, assess project requirements, prepare quotations and continue the requested business conversation. Submitting an enquiry does not require you to accept analytics or advertising tracking.

Service Providers

Cloudflare Pages hosts the website and processes requests needed to serve and protect it. The RFQ form sends enquiry details through a Cloudflare Function to Resend, which delivers the message to the STEELLCX sales email address. This release does not load Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 or Google advertising-measurement technologies. Links to WhatsApp open a separate service subject to its own privacy practices.

Cookies and Optional Tracking

Optional Google analytics and advertising tags are disabled in this release. The website does not load them or use a previously saved tracking choice. A previous version may have set Google cookies; you can clear them through your browser controls. The RFQ form works without optional tracking.

Retention and Security

We retain RFQ enquiry details only while needed to respond, continue project discussions, maintain necessary business records, or meet applicable legal requirements. We will review them and delete or anonymise them when no longer needed. We do not promise a fixed retention period or claim an automated deletion system. Resend's provider-side retention does not determine how long enquiries remain in our sales mailbox. Contact us for information about your enquiry data. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices

Optional Google website tracking is disabled in this release. If a prior visit set Google cookies, you can clear them using browser controls. Contact sales@steellcx.com to request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, where applicable. Specific rights depend on your location.

International Processing

Resend states that it stores customer data, including email content and logs, in the United States. Its published retention varies by plan; our actual plan and account DPA status have not yet been verified. Cloudflare operates a global network; this site's specific processing locations, data-localisation settings and transfer arrangements have not yet been verified. We do not claim a single Cloudflare processing country or a confirmed account-specific transfer safeguard.

Updates

We will update this notice if the website's data practices or approved consent arrangements change.
