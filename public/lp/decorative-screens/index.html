FAQ

Questions About Custom Screens

Can you manufacture from CAD or PDF drawings?

Yes. Send us your drawing, dimensions or reference design for technical review and quotation.

Can size and pattern be customized?

Yes. Screen dimensions, pattern, frame structure and finish can be customized according to project requirements.

What stainless steel grades are available?

304 stainless steel is commonly used for interior applications, while 316 can be supplied when the project requires higher corrosion resistance.

Can you provide PVD finishes?

Yes. PVD and other decorative stainless steel finishes are available depending on the project specification.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We support international project shipments and can discuss packing and shipping requirements during quotation.
