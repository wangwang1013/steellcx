Privacy Policy

This policy explains how STEELLCX handles information submitted through this website when you request a quotation or contact us about a project.

Information We Collect

When you submit an RFQ or enquiry, we may collect your name, company, email address, WhatsApp or phone number, country or region, project type and project details.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, discuss customer projects and communicate about requested products or services.

Service Providers

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages infrastructure. RFQ enquiries are delivered by Resend as transactional email to the STEELLCX sales team. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Retention and Security

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, manage related business communications and meet applicable obligations. We use reasonable measures to protect submitted information, but no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us at sales@steellcx.com to ask about information you have submitted, including requests to correct or delete it where applicable.

Cookies and Tracking

This website does not currently use analytics or advertising tracking tools. If this changes, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when website practices change. The current version is published on this page.
